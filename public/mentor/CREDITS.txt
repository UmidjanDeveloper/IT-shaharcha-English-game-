Current observatory and instructor
  instructor.glb is a custom, fully surfaced character based on the provided likeness and generated front, profile and rear references. Face depth was fitted with the MediaPipe canonical face reference (Google, Apache License 2.0):
  https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/mediapipe/modules/face_geometry/data/canonical_face_model.obj
  License copy: /teacher/mediapipe-license.txt
  The character has 22 joints and three retargeted animation clips derived from the Cranberry capture credited above. Rotations have been adapted to the instructor's neutral stance and sampled at 15 fps.
  The pavilion and furnishings are original geometry. Distant foothills are generated scenery. Globe coastlines derive from Natural Earth public-domain land data: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
