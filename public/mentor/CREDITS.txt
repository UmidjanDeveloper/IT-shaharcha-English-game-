3D teacher
  instructor.glb — the club's instructor, a custom character based on the owner's own photo (provided by
  the owner for this purpose). Face depth was fitted with the MediaPipe canonical face reference
  (Google, Apache License 2.0), license copy: /teacher/mediapipe-license.txt.
  In the browser, paper-thin triangles at the limb ends are removed at load so they cannot stretch into lines.

Teacher motion
  Motion capture from AI4AnimationPy, Paul Starke and Sebastian Starke, Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates.
  Source: https://github.com/facebookresearch/ai4animationpy — Cranberry Sh06 standing_S01 capture.
  License: CC BY-NC 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) — non-commercial use.
  Idle, explaining and listening takes; blinking, lip movement, gaze, waving and presenting added in code.

Interface animations
  BlurText, RotatingText, ShinyText, CountUp and Magnet from React Bits by David Haz
  (https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits), MIT + Commons Clause — src/vendor/react-bits/LICENSE.md.
  Scroll reveal and tilt cards: own implementation inspired by adrianhajdin/project_3D_developer_portfolio.
